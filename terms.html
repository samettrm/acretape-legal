1. What AcreTape does

AcreTape measures area, perimeter and distance from points you place on a map or record with your device's GPS. Results depend on the accuracy of your device, the satellite signal and the map imagery. AcreTape shows the reported GPS accuracy while you measure. AcreTape is a measuring tool; it does not replace a licensed land survey, a cadastral record or a legal boundary determination.
