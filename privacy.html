Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

AcreTape: GPS Area Calculator (com.busater.acretape, “AcreTape”, “we”) is published by BUSATER YAZILIM A.Ş. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, who receives it and what you can do about it. AcreTape shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK.

1. Data that stays on your device

MeasurementsThe points you place or record, area, perimeter, names, groups, notes and marked points. Stored in the app's own database on your phone.
PhotosPhotos you add to a measurement. They are copied into the app's storage and are never uploaded.
LocationYour precise location is used only while the app is open and you are measuring or viewing the map. It is saved only as the measurement points you choose to keep. AcreTape does not use your location in the background.
Offline mapsMap regions you download are stored on the device.

2. Data that leaves your device

Account (optional)If you sign in with Google or Apple: your email address and the sign-in identifier from that provider. Measuring and saving work without an account.
Sync (optional, AcreTape Pro)If you are signed in and turn on sync: your saved measurements (points, area, names, groups, notes and marked points — not photos) are stored in your account so they appear on your other devices. Only you can read them.
PurchasesPayments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card. RevenueCat keeps the status of your AcreTape Pro purchase, linked to an anonymous app user ID (or your account ID if you are signed in) and a random installation ID.
App usage statisticsA random installation ID created by the app, app version, Android version, device type (phone or tablet), device model, country (from the network or your device's region setting), language, and in-app events such as “measurement saved”, “PDF created” or “paywall viewed”, plus whether the app crashed. This contains no names, notes, coordinates or email addresses. We use it to count users, understand which features are used and fix problems. It is never used for advertising.
Update notificationsIf you allow notifications: a push token, app version and language, used only to tell you that a new version is available.
Map tiles and Mapbox telemetryMaps are provided by Mapbox. To draw the map your device requests map tiles from Mapbox. The Mapbox SDK also sends unidentified location and usage data to Mapbox (“Mapbox Telemetry”). You can turn this off at any time: tap the ⓘ button on the map and choose the telemetry setting.

3. Service providers

SupabaseStores accounts, synced measurements and usage statistics. Servers in the European Union (Frankfurt).
RevenueCatManages the status of AcreTape Pro purchases.
Google PlayApp distribution, payments and in-app updates.
Google / Apple sign-inOnly if you choose to sign in.
Expo (push service)Delivers update notifications.
MapboxMap tiles and telemetry, see section 2.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone for advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account and synced measurements are deleted at once; usage statistics are kept only in a form no longer linked to you. More options: Delete your account.
  • Usage statistics that are not linked to an account are kept for up to 24 months.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA and Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK) you may ask to access, correct, delete or export your data and object to its processing. Write to us at the address below; we answer within 30 days.

6. Children

AcreTape is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way we will say so in the app. The current version is always on this page.
